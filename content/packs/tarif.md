## How-to cards

### Buy a bus ticket

1. Download the IDS BK app (or Cyril) — e-tickets are cheaper: 30 min €1.09.
2. Choose 30 min for a short trip, 60 min to the centre. Activate before you board.
3. Paper ticket from the yellow machine? Validate it in the orange box on the bus.
4. Monthly pass €40.50 — ask reception for the form; your employer may reimburse it.

### Register with a doctor

1. Ask your employer's HR for your health-insurance card (VšZP, Dôvera or Union).
2. Find a GP (“všeobecný lekár”) taking new patients — reception has a list.
3. Bring passport, residence card and insurance card; sign the registration form.
4. Urgent problem? Go to urgent care (pohotovosť) or call 155.

### Send money home

1. Bring your passport and the receiver's full name.
2. Compare the app price with the counter price — apps are usually cheaper.
3. Keep the receipt until the money arrives.

### Get a Slovak SIM

1. Orange, Telekom, O2 or 4ka shops at Shopping Palace.
2. Bring your passport — prepaid SIMs are registered to you.
3. Top up in the operator's app, at machines or kiosks.

### Your residence permit

1. Carry your residence card and passport.
2. Renewal: apply before expiry — the app reminds you 90, 60 and 30 days ahead.
3. Address change: report to the foreign police — reception helps with the form.
4. Questions: IOM MIC gives free advice in Ukrainian and Russian.
